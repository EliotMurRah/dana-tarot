Questions

Things people often ask.

Do I need to believe in tarot for it to work?

No. Think of the cards as a beautiful mirror. Whether you see them as spiritual, psychological, or simply a way to slow down and reflect, the conversation we have around them is what matters.

Can you tell me the future?

I read the energy of where things are heading — not a fixed script. You always have a say in what comes next. That’s the whole point.

What if I don’t have a specific question?

That’s completely fine. Many of the most meaningful readings start with “I just feel like I need this.” We’ll find the thread together.

How do online readings work?

We meet on a video call. I shuffle and lay the cards on camera, and we talk through them just as we would in person. You’ll receive a photo of your spread afterwards.

Is everything we talk about private?

Always. What’s shared in a reading stays in the reading.
