Questions

Before you come.

Do I need to believe in tarot for it to work?

No. Think of the cards as a beautiful mirror. Whether you see them as spiritual, psychological, or simply a way to slow down and reflect, the conversation around them is what matters.

Can Dana tell me the future?

She reads the energy of where things are heading — not a fixed script. You always have a say in what comes next. That is the whole point.

What if I don’t have a specific question?

Completely fine. Many of the most meaningful readings start with “I just feel like I need this.” You’ll find the thread together.

How do online readings work?

You meet on a video call. Dana shuffles and lays the cards on camera, and you talk through them just as you would in person. You receive a photo of your spread afterwards.

Is everything we talk about private?

Always. What is shared in a reading stays in the reading.
